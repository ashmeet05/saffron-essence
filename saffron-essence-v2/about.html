About us

Saffron Essence opened in 2018 with one idea: cook the food we grew up eating, the way our families made it. Every curry starts from whole spices we toast and grind in the kitchen each morning.

Our menu travels across India, from Punjabi butter chicken to Bengali rasgulla, with a few modern touches. What started as one dining room in Brampton is now eight kitchens across Ontario, from Ottawa to London, each cooking the same recipes from scratch.

What we care about

  • Real recipesTraditional dishes, cooked from scratch every day.
  • Good ingredientsFresh produce, whole spices and halal meat.
  • Vegetarian choiceMost of our menu is meat-free, and it's marked clearly.
  • Less wasteCompostable containers and food donated at closing.

The team

Our kitchen is led by Chef Asha, who trained in Delhi before moving to Canada. The whole team treats every order as if it were going to their own family table.
